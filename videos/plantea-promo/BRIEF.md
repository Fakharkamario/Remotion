# Plantea promo (HyperFrames)

**Message:** Another dead plant? Scan it, get a diagnosis and a care plan — keep your plants alive.

**Source:** Recreation of a 10.3s reference app promo (1920x1080, 30fps). Scene cuts, durations and
motion curves were matched frame-by-frame to the reference; all artwork is original (the Plantea
vector plant/room illustrations from the Remotion project, via `scripts/gen-art.mjs`), and the
brand is Plantea.

| # | Scene | Start | Dur | Motion |
|---|-------|-------|-----|--------|
| 1 | intro | 0.000 | 1.700 | "Another" slam-in, plant tiles orbit CCW, "dead plant?" blooms green → ink, tiles collapse into the gap |
| 2 | room | 1.700 | 2.467 | slow push, glow sweep, glass "Analyze" pill types in, hard punch-in at 3.167s, press |
| 3 | scan | 4.167 | 1.500 | card settle + slide left, scan line up/down, "Scanning plant…" assembles |
| 4 | diagnosis | 5.667 | 0.933 | "Diagnosis ready" drift, green → ink |
| 5 | care-card | 6.600 | 1.033 | rack-focus care card |
| 6 | rooms-tagline | 7.633 | 2.033 | whip pan through healthy rooms with velocity motion blur, tagline panel lands |
| 7 | logo | 9.667 | 0.633 | Plantea wordmark |

Audio reuses the Remotion project's synthesized music bed and SFX (`public/audio`).
