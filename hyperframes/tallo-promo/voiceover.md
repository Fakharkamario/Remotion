# Tallo promo: voiceover script

14.7 s at 30 fps. Times are when each line should start in the video. Lines marked **on screen** match text the viewer reads, so land each one with its text. Lines marked *bridge* cover shots with no text; they're optional, but keep them under the word budget so the next on-screen line still lands on time.

## Plain text for a TTS tool

Paste this as one block. The ellipses and full stops give the pauses the timing needs.

> We've all been there. Rent, groceries, subscriptions… another bill slips by. Late fees. Again. What if your money just… sorted itself? Budget. Bills. Savings. All in one place. Smart. Simple. Secure. Tallo. tallo.app.

## Pacing notes

- The whole read is about 30 words in 14.7 s, roughly 2 words per second: calm and unhurried, like the reference.
- "What if your money just…" and "…sorted itself?" are one sentence split across two shots. Hold the pause on "just" (about 0.5 s) so "sorted" lands as the new line flies in at 6.0 s.
- "Smart. Simple. Secure." come about half a second apart. Give each word its own beat.
- If the generated read runs long, drop the bridge lines first. Every on-screen line still lines up.
