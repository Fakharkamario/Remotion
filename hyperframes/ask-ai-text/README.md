# Ask AI — headline card

A 2.5 s, 30 fps headline in the same paper, ink and Inter style as the other acend clips. "Ask" grows, then "AI" joins. A dark pill grows behind "AI" and the word turns paper-white. The line keeps a slow push, then the letters lift away from left to right.

- Preview: `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.114 preview`
- Render with motion blur: `scripts/render-mb.sh` (outputs `renders/ask-ai-text.mp4`)
