## Facts (sources: US Senate Inquiry (1912) and British Wreck Commissioner's Inquiry (1912) testimony;
## Walter Lord, *A Night to Remember* (1955); Encyclopedia Titanica timeline)
- Struck the iceberg at 11:40 pm, 14 April 1912. Many passengers felt only a slight jar or grinding; some went back to bed.
- Lifeboats were ordered uncovered around 12:05 am; stewards then roused passengers and sent them up with lifebelts.
- Thomas Andrews told Captain Smith the ship would sink in an hour or two, so the officers knew before most passengers did.
- The band played lively ragtime on deck to keep people calm.
- The first lifeboat (No. 7) left at about 12:45 with ~28 people in a boat for 65. "Women and children first."
- 20 lifeboats with room for 1,178 people; 2,224 aboard, so seats for about half.
- Moonless, flat-calm night; the calm sea and no moon made the iceberg hard to see.
- The lights failed and the ship broke apart around 2:18 am; it sank at 2:20.
- Water temperature about 28 °F (−2 °C). About 1,500 died; ~710 survived. Boats left with hundreds of empty seats.
