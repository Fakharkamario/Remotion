Good to know

How long does a video take?

Most focused product films take around 2–3 weeks from kickoff to final delivery. Faster timelines can be scoped, and our fastest turnaround so far is 24 hours.

Do you handle the script and concept?

Yes. We can take the project from positioning, concept and script through storyboard, edit, motion, sound and final delivery.

What do you need from us?

Usually the product, your brand assets, access to the relevant screens and a clear goal. We help structure everything else.

Can you make multiple versions?

Yes. Cutdowns, social versions, alternate aspect ratios and extra exports can all be part of the scope.

Where are you based?

We're a small remote studio based in Pakistan, working with product teams worldwide. 200+ projects across 25+ markets so far.
