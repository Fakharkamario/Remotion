How a launch actually runs

Brief to launch day.
No mystery handoffs.

  1. 01 / 04

    Discover

    One kickoff call and your assets. We learn how the product works, who it's for and what makes it different, then come back with a creative direction and a plan.

    Days 1–3
  2. 02 / 04

    Shape

    Script first. Your approval second. Storyboard and visual direction third. Nothing gets animated until the story holds up on paper.

    Week 1
  3. 03 / 04

    Make

    Edit, motion, sound and product UI come together in one controlled production, with focused feedback rounds instead of endless threads.

    Week 2
  4. 04 / 04

    Polish & ship

    We refine every frame, cut the versions you need and hand over a launch-ready package for your site, socials, ads and deck.

    Week 3 · Launch

How long does a video take?

Most focused product films take around 2–3 weeks from kickoff to final delivery. Faster timelines can be scoped when you need them, and our fastest turnaround so far is 24 hours.

Do you handle the script and concept?

Yes. We can take the project from positioning, concept and script through storyboard, edit, motion, sound and final delivery.

What do you need from us?

Usually the product, your brand assets, access to the relevant screens and a clear goal. We help structure everything else.

Can you make multiple versions?

Yes. Cutdowns, social versions, alternate aspect ratios and additional exports can all be part of the scope.

How does pricing work?

The estimator above shows transparent starting rates for common scopes. Your final price is confirmed after we agree on the brief, creative direction and timeline. No surprises after that.

Where are you based?

We're a remote studio based in Pakistan, working with product teams worldwide across 25+ markets.
